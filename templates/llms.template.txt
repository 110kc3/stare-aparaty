# Stare Aparaty

> Curated Polish-language catalog of second-hand analog (film) cameras sold by the site owner, plus hand-picked film stocks and developing/scanning gear. The camera catalog is refreshed daily from the owner's live OLX listings — each entry carries title, price, availability and a direct purchase link. Page content is in Polish.

## Machine-readable data

- Camera catalog (JSON, rebuilt daily): https://stareaparaty.com/olx_meta.json — array of {id, title, image, url, host, sold, price, oldPrice, firstSeen, etag, lastModified}; `sold: true` means the listing is no longer available, `firstSeen` is the ISO date the listing entered the catalog (empty for listings that predate the field), and `etag`/`lastModified` are HTTP validators used internally for conditional refetching
- Film price data (JSON): https://stareaparaty.com/amazon-products.json (keyed by Amazon ASIN) and https://stareaparaty.com/allegro-products.json (keyed by slug); both carry label, price in PLN and lastChecked date
- Structured data: schema.org WebSite + ItemList of Product/Offer nodes embedded as JSON-LD in https://stareaparaty.com/
- Everything in one fetch: https://stareaparaty.com/llms-full.txt — this file with the full camera catalog (title, price, availability, link) inlined, rebuilt alongside the catalog
- Agent card: https://stareaparaty.com/.well-known/agent-card.json (A2A 1.0 path; the pre-0.3 path https://stareaparaty.com/.well-known/agent.json serves the same card)
- Sitemap: https://stareaparaty.com/sitemap.xml

## Notes

- Camera links go to live OLX listings by the owner; film and accessory links are Amazon/Allegro affiliate links.
